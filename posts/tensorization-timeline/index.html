ASVD

Yuan et al.

First to compress LLM weights by decomposition: scales each matrix by an activation-derived diagonal before SVD, and shrinks the KV-cache by factorizing K/V projections.

FacT — Factor-Tuning

Jie & Deng · AAAI

Aggregates ViT weights into one 3D tensor and tunes only the decomposed increment (TT or Tucker). Matches SOTA at a fraction of the trainable parameters.

TT-LoRA & LoRETTA

Anjum et al. (LANL) · Yang et al. (NAACL)

Decompose the LoRA update ΔW directly into Tensor-Train cores — no adapter matrices. A 768×2304 ΔW collapses from 1.77M to 1,135 params.

LoTR

Represents adapters across many layers jointly as one tensor decomposition with shared factors — cost O(Lr²+dr) decouples depth from width.

The Decomposition Toolkit

Ordered by structural complexity — each family has an architecture where it works best.

SVD

Low-rank matrix factorization

The order-2 base case, W ≈ UΣVᵀ. Tucker-1 is identical to SVD.

Home: LLM weights

CP

CANDECOMP / PARAFAC

Sum of rank-one tensors — most aggressive, but unstable & hard to rank.

Home: Conv kernels

Tucker

Core tensor × factor matrices

The robust default; Tucker-2 on channel modes is the conv workhorse.

Home: CNNs

TT / MPO

Tensor-Train / Matrix Product Operator

A chain of 3-index cores, O(dnr²) — identical to the MPO of physics.

Home: FC, embeddings, transformers

TR

Tensor Ring

TT with periodic boundary — more flexible rank allocation.

Home: Wide conv / FC

HT

Hierarchical Tucker

Binary tree of cores; tops the charts for recurrent nets.

Home: RNN / LSTM

BTD

Block-Term Decomposition

A sum of Tucker blocks; interpolates between CP and Tucker.

Home: Multi-head attention

Kronecker

W ≈ A ⊗ B

Structured products for RNN (KCP) and LLM weight compression.

Home: RNN / LLM

The Through-Line

One arc runs the whole decade: the same low-rank idea migrates to whatever layer is currently the biggest — from convolution kernels, to fully-connected matrices, to attention, and finally to the colossal weight matrices of language models, where it now meets quantization and pruning as one composable axis of compression.

2015–18 Kernels & FC 2017–20 Sequence models 2019–22 Attention 2023–26 LLMs & PEFT

Vendor figures (CompactifAI / Multiverse) are company-reported. FLOPs reductions do not always equal wall-clock speedups, and most strong results assume a fine-tuning / "healing" step.
